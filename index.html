From Chapter 5: Poultry & Meat

Mediterranean Lemon Herb Chicken & Roasted Vegetables

Prep: 15 min
Cook: 25 min
4 Servings
Ingredients
  • 600g chicken breast, cut into bite-sized cubes
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • Juice and zest of 1 unwaxed lemon
  • 2 cloves garlic, finely minced
  • 1 tsp dried oregano & 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 medium zucchini, sliced into half-moons
  • 1 bell pepper (any color), deseeded and chopped
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • Sea salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
Method
  1. 1 Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F) and line a large baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. 2 In a bowl, whisk olive oil, lemon juice, lemon zest, minced garlic, oregano, thyme, salt, and pepper.
  3. 3 Spread the chopped zucchini, bell pepper, cherry tomatoes, and chicken cubes across the tray in a single layer. Drizzle the herb marinade evenly over everything and toss gently to coat.
  4. 4 Roast for 22 to 25 minutes until the chicken is cooked through and the vegetables are tender with golden roasted edges. Serve warm with lemon wedges.
